## linkedin-post-writer — Cal is a good fit (coach + physiologist + business owner)
```
I've coached triathletes for 30 years.

The most common mistake I see isn't a lack of fitness. It's pacing.

Athletes run their brick sessions off the bike as hard as they can, then fade on race day.
When we tested it, most of them ran their best brick runs at about 95% of their fresh 10K pace.

Slower in training. Faster on race day.

What's the training habit you had to unlearn?
```
⚠️ Line 3 is a not-X-but-Y contrast. The humanizer would rewrite it to: "The most common mistake I see is pacing."
Link to criticalspeed.com goes in the first comment.
