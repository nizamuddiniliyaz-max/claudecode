## 04 /ig-carousel — "Your brick run is too fast" (8 slides, rendered → `04-carousel/`)
1. **Your brick run is too fast** · How to pace the run off the bike.
2. **Why it matters** · Hammer the brick and you train a pace you can't hold on race day.
3. **Find your fresh 10K pace** · Use a recent 10K race or test. Say 5:00 per km.
4. **Take 95% of it** · 5:00 ÷ 0.95 ≈ 5:15 per km. That's your brick pace.
5. **Hold it 15–20 minutes** · Long enough to learn the pace. Short enough to recover.
6. **Easy first 2 km** · Let your legs come back from the bike. Then settle in.
7. **Recap** · fresh 10K pace / × 0.95 (slower) / 15–20 min / easy first 2 km
8. **Save this** · Use it on your next brick.
