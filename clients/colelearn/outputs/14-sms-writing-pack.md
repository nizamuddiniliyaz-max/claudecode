## hook-writer — 6 hooks, 6 different mechanisms (scored with Jake's hookscore.py)
| Score | Mechanism | Hook | Truth check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 77.8 | Identity | If you train for HYROX twice a day, watch this first. | ✅ |
| 74.2 | Specificity | The station I'd train if I only had 20 minutes a week. | {{Cole picks the station}} |
| 67.4 | Contrarian | Your wall balls aren't the problem. The 1K run before them is. | ✅ opinion |
| 59.0 | Stakes | Why most HYROX athletes lose 3 minutes in the Roxzone. | ❌ "3 minutes" is invented, cut it |
| 49.2 | Authority | 49:13. Here's every split from the Mixed Doubles world record. | ✅ needs real splits |
| 47.1 | Story | I was homeless in Edmonton. Ten years later I hold a HYROX world record. | ✅ public record, Cole approves |

**Finding:** Jake's scorer ranks the story hook *last* (47). Human judgment says it's Cole's strongest
hook: it's what the podcast and press lead with. The scorer rewards formula shapes and penalises
narrative hooks. Don't let it veto a story hook.

## caption-writer vs /ig-caption
caption-writer gave 3 angles (value / story / question) for the same Reel. ig-caption gave one, linted.
- Option A (value): same as ig-caption v2.
- Option B (story): `Ten years ago I didn't know if I'd wake up some days. Now I coach people to race HYROX on an hour a day.` {{Cole approves}}
- Option C (question): `How many sessions a week are you doing for HYROX? Be honest.`
**Verdict:** caption-writer is better for *choosing an angle*; ig-caption is better for *finishing* (its linter caught real issues).
